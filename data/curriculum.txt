Equip farmers with the knowledge and skills to properly handle and store cashew nuts and apples after harvesting, ensuring quality preservation and reducing post-harvest losses while adhering to organic standards.

6.2 Storage Methods for Organic Produce

6.3 Enhancing cashew nut quality through preferred drying techniques (For example: air drying is better than sun drying)

6.4 Compliance with Organic Standards
